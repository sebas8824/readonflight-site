Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Read on Flight collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your books, highlights, notes and questions stay on your iPhone, and the developer never receives them.

What stays on your iPhone

The books you import, and everything you make from them: highlights, notes, topics, study projects, conversations with Atlas, and the search index built from your books. They are stored inside the app on your device and protected by iOS data protection.

Your library is included in the backups your iPhone makes to iCloud or to a computer, if you have backups turned on. Those backups are governed by your own settings and by Apple. Book covers and the search index are left out of backups, because the app can rebuild them.

Atlas

Atlas answers questions, distills and improves notes using Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). Your questions, the passages it reads and its answers are processed on your iPhone and are not sent anywhere.

Dictation

If you dictate a note or a question, the app asks for access to the microphone and to speech recognition. It listens only while you are dictating, and recognition runs entirely on your iPhone. The audio is not sent anywhere. You can turn this access off at any time in Settings.

Network connections

The app makes no network connections of its own and never downloads anything a book refers to. There are only two exceptions, and you start both:

  • Purchases. Buying or restoring the Lifetime unlock goes through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment under Apple's privacy policy. The developer receives no personal or payment information; the app only learns whether Lifetime is unlocked.
  • Links in a book. If you tap a web link inside a book, the app shows you the full address first. Only if you confirm does it open in your web browser, outside the app.

Crash and performance reports

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer anonymous crash and performance reports. They contain no books, notes or questions. You can turn this off in the same place.

Files you export

When you export notes or highlights, the file goes where you choose, such as Files or another app. What happens to it after that is up to you and that app.

Deleting your data

Deleting a book in the app deletes its file. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone. Copies in your device backups are removed when those backups are replaced or deleted.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
