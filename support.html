Help with Read on Flight

Get in touch

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue. Include your iPhone model, its iOS version and what you were doing. Please do not attach books or notes you would not want to make public: issues are visible to everyone.

Requirements

Read on Flight runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Reading, highlighting, notes, topics and search work on every supported iPhone.

Atlas needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On an iPhone without it, Atlas does not appear and there is nothing to buy.

Atlas and the Lifetime unlock

  • You can try Atlas ten times for free. Answering a question, distilling notes and improving a note each count as one use, once it completes.
  • Lifetime is a single purchase that unlocks Atlas for good, with no subscription.
  • Atlas answers from your books, but it can also use general knowledge, and it can be wrong. Check what matters against the book.

Restore a purchase

After reinstalling the app or moving to a new iPhone, open Atlas's unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Refunds

Purchases are made through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

The app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
